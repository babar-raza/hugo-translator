## General Questions

**Q1. What kind of products does Aspose offer?**  
Aspose offers APIs, SDKs, and plugins focused on file format processing—including creation, editing, conversion, and rendering for formats like DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, PDF, images, and more.  
This is summarized on the public company overview at [about.aspose.com](https://about.aspose.com/), which lists supported families such as Words, Cells, Slides, PDF, Email, and others.

**Q2. Do Aspose products require Microsoft Office or other third‑party tools?**  
No. The company overview explicitly states that Aspose components are self‑sufficient and do not depend on Microsoft Office, OpenOffice, or similar suites.  
This design allows deployment on servers and cloud platforms where installing desktop office software is not feasible.

## Licensing and Support

**Q3. How are Aspose products licensed?**  
Licensing information is described on the company and purchase pages, where Aspose offers traditional licenses as well as metered licensing for some APIs.  
Details and FAQs about licensing options are available on the [Aspose purchase portal](https://purchase.aspose.com/).

**Q4. What support options are available?**  
Aspose provides free technical support via public forums, along with sales assistance and live chat channels referenced on the contact pages.  
The “Contact Us” and overview sections on [about.aspose.com](https://about.aspose.com/) and [purchase.aspose.com](https://purchase.aspose.com/) outline these options.

## Company Background

**Q5. When was Aspose founded and where is it based?**  
The company overview notes that Aspose was founded in 2002 and is headquartered in Australia, with a globally distributed team and customer base.  
This background information comes from the “Our story” section of the public overview at [about.aspose.com](https://about.aspose.com/).

**Q6. Which industries use Aspose products?**  
Customer stories and marketing pages highlight usage across finance, healthcare, education, government, and software vendors, among others.  
Representative customer success stories are linked from the [Success Stories](https://about.aspose.com/customers/success-stories/) page.
