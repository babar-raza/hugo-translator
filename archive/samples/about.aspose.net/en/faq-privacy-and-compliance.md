## Personal Data and Purpose

**Q1. What personal information does Aspose collect?**  
The Privacy Policy explains that Aspose may collect your name, company name, email address, and similar details when you register, download products, or request services.  
Technical information such as IP address, browser type, and usage patterns may also be collected for analytics and security, as described in the “Information We Collect” section at [about.aspose.com/legal/privacy-policy/](https://about.aspose.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

**Q2. For what purposes is this data used?**  
Collected data is used to provide and improve Aspose services, manage licenses and accounts, process orders, and communicate important updates.  
The policy notes that information is also used to enhance website performance and maintain security.

## GDPR and User Rights

**Q3. Is Aspose compliant with GDPR and similar regulations?**  
Yes. The policy describes Aspose’s commitment to applicable data protection laws, including GDPR, and outlines responsibilities as a data controller for user information.  
Details on legal bases for processing and user rights are provided in the GDPR‑related sections of the Privacy Policy.

**Q4. How can I access, update, or delete my personal data?**  
Users can contact Aspose through the channels listed in the Privacy Policy or on the contact page to request access, correction, or deletion of stored personal data.  
The policy explains how these requests are handled and under which circumstances data may be retained for legal or operational reasons.

## Security, Cookies, and Changes

**Q5. How does Aspose protect stored information?**  
The Privacy Policy states that Aspose treats stored information as confidential and limits access to authorized staff.  
It mentions security measures designed to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration of personal data.

**Q6. Does Aspose use cookies or tracking technologies?**  
Yes. The policy includes a section describing cookie usage and similar technologies for analytics and personalization.  
Users are advised to review cookie‑related details and can adjust browser settings or use provided controls where applicable.

**Q7. How will I know if the Privacy Policy changes?**  
Any updates to the Privacy Policy are reflected in the “Last Updated” date at the top of the document.  
Aspose recommends that users periodically review the policy text at [about.aspose.com/legal/privacy-policy/](https://about.aspose.com/legal/privacy-policy/) to stay informed.
