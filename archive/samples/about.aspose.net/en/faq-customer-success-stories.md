## About the Stories

**Q1. What is a customer success story at Aspose?**  
A customer success story describes how an organization used Aspose products to solve a real business problem—for example, automating document generation or accelerating spreadsheet processing.  
The public [Success Stories](https://about.aspose.com/customers/success-stories/) page provides representative examples across multiple product families.

**Q2. Which Aspose products are featured in success stories?**  
Collections exist for product families such as Aspose.Total, Aspose.Cells, Aspose.Words, Aspose.PDF, Aspose.Slides, Aspose.BarCode, Aspose.Email, Aspose.Tasks, Aspose.HTML, Aspose.Imaging, and Aspose.PSD.  
Links to these collections are listed on the Success Stories page and point to detailed case studies hosted on the Conholdate library.

## Participation and Submission

**Q3. How can my organization submit a success story?**  
The Success Stories page explains that customers can submit their experiences by contacting Aspose sales.  
A link is provided to the [Contact](https://about.aspose.com/contact) page, where you can reach the sales team with details of your scenario.

**Q4. Are there benefits to sharing a story?**  
According to the Success Stories page, sharing your experience helps other developers evaluate Aspose products and may qualify you for rewards or promotional opportunities.  
Specific incentives can be confirmed with the sales team when you submit your story.

## Use of Customer Information

**Q5. How are customer names and quotes used in success stories?**  
Published success stories typically include the customer’s name, organization, and a short quote describing their experience using Aspose products.  
These details are taken from submissions and are published with the customer’s consent, consistent with Aspose’s [Privacy Policy](https://about.aspose.com/legal/privacy-policy/).
