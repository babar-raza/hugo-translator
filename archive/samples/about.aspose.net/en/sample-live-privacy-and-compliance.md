## Scope of the Privacy Policy

Aspose’s public Privacy Policy explains how the company collects, uses, and protects personal data for visitors and users of its websites, SaaS products, downloadable software, and mobile apps.  
The policy defines terms such as “Visitor,” “User,” and “Service,” clarifying that the same principles apply across online and installed offerings.

The document makes it clear that by accessing Aspose websites or services, users agree to the terms of the Privacy Policy.  
If they do not agree, they are instructed not to use the services, ensuring transparent consent aligned with modern privacy expectations.

You can read the full policy text at [about.aspose.com/legal/privacy-policy/](https://about.aspose.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

## Data Collection and Usage

According to the policy, Aspose collects limited personal information when users register, download products, or request services.  
Typical fields include name, company name, and email address, along with technical data such as IP address and browser details used for analytics and security.

The policy explains that this information is used for account management, license delivery, customer communication, and improvement of products and services.  
It also clarifies that Aspose treats stored information as confidential and restricts access to authorized personnel only, aligning with industry best practices.

These details are described in the “Information We Collect” and related sections of the Privacy Policy.

## Compliance, Rights, and Security

The Privacy Policy outlines Aspose’s commitment to GDPR and similar regulations, including the rights of individuals to access, correct, or delete their personal data.  
It explains how users can contact Aspose to exercise these rights or ask questions about data protection.

Additional sections describe cookie usage, data retention, and the security measures used to protect stored information.  
Aspose encourages users to review these sections regularly, as the policy may be updated over time with a revised “Last Updated” date.

For the latest compliance details, see the full policy at [about.aspose.com/legal/privacy-policy/](https://about.aspose.com/legal/privacy-policy/).
