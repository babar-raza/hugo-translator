## General Questions

**Q1. What is the Aspose for .NET Knowledge Base?**  
The KB is a collection of focused articles that show how to accomplish specific tasks with Aspose for .NET plugins, such as adding images or watermarks to documents.  
It complements the more comprehensive product documentation available on [docs.aspose.com](https://docs.aspose.com/).

**Q2. How is the KB organized?**  
Articles are grouped by product family—Words, Cells, PDF, HTML, and others—and often tagged by scenario or technology.  
This structure is visible in the repository under paths like `content/kb.aspose.net/words/en/` and in the category navigation on [kb.aspose.com](https://kb.aspose.com/).

## Content and Use Cases

**Q3. What types of articles can I expect?**  
Most KB entries are step‑by‑step tutorials or troubleshooting guides, frequently including prerequisites, numbered steps, and C# code samples.  
Examples include “How to Add Images in Word Documents Using C#” and “How to Add Watermarks to Word Documents via ASP.NET Core REST API.”

**Q4. How does KB content differ from the main documentation?**  
KB articles focus on solving a single problem end‑to‑end, whereas the documentation provides broader conceptual and reference material.  
KB pages will often link back to API reference and conceptual articles when additional context is needed.

## Updates and Feedback

**Q5. How often is KB content updated?**  
KB articles are updated alongside product releases and customer feedback, especially when new features or best practices emerge.  
Date and last‑modified metadata in the article front matter indicate when content was last refreshed.

**Q6. How can I request a new KB article?**  
Users can suggest new topics through support forums or contact channels referenced on Aspose websites.  
Feedback on existing articles can also be provided so that the KB team can refine or expand coverage over time.
