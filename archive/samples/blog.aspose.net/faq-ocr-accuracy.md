## Preprocessing and Image Quality

**Q1. Why is preprocessing important for OCR?**
The article explains that cleaning up images through deskewing, noise reduction, and contrast adjustments can dramatically improve recognition accuracy.
Poor-quality scans often require such preprocessing before OCR delivers reliable results.

**Q2. Which preprocessing operations are commonly recommended?**
Techniques mentioned include deskewing, binarization, resizing, and removing background noise.
These operations can be performed using Aspose.OCR or complementary imaging APIs prior to recognition.

## Configuration and Tuning

**Q3. How can I tune Aspose.OCR for specific languages or layouts?**
You can select recognition languages, adjust recognition modes, and specify regions of interest to focus on relevant text.
The article notes that language-specific models and layout-aware settings can reduce errors.

**Q4. How should I evaluate improvements in OCR accuracy?**
The article recommends comparing results before and after applying preprocessing and configuration changes, using representative sample documents.
Metrics such as character or word accuracy can help quantify improvements.
