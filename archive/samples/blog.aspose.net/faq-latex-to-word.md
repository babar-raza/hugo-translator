## Using the Online Converter

**Q1. Do I need to install anything to use the converter?**
No.
The article emphasizes that the LaTeX to Word converter is completely online and requires no installation or signup.
You simply upload your `.tex` or `.ltx` file in the browser and start the conversion.

**Q2. Which file types can I upload?**
The article states that you can upload `.tex` or `.ltx` files.
Support for associated assets such as images is handled when you upload projects with related resources.

**Q3. Will equations and images be preserved in the DOCX output?**
Yes.
The article highlights that the converter is designed to keep equations, images, and structure intact as much as possible.
