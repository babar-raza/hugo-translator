## Capabilities and Output

**Q1. What does the Document Printer plugin do?**
It provides APIs for printing Word documents from .NET applications with high fidelity, leveraging the Aspose.Words rendering engine.
The product page describes scenarios such as batch printing of invoices, statements, and other business documents.

**Q2. Can the plugin print to both physical and virtual printers?**
Yes. The underlying rendering APIs support printing to standard Windows printers as well as virtual devices like PDF or XPS drivers.
Configuration examples are referenced from the product page and associated documentation.

## Integration and Configuration

**Q3. How do I integrate printing into my application?**
Typical steps include loading a document with `Document`, configuring printer and page settings, and invoking print or render methods.
These workflows are summarized in the Document Printer plugin’s product content at `content/products.aspose.net/words/en/document-printer/_index.md`.

**Q4. Is it suitable for server-side batch printing?**
Yes. The plugin is designed for automated, server-side printing and is commonly used in batch processes.
Guidance on deployment and performance considerations is provided in the Word Printer-related documentation on [docs.aspose.com](https://docs.aspose.com/words/net/).

## Licensing

**Q5. How is the Document Printer plugin licensed?**
Licensing is managed through the Aspose.Words plugin family, with options outlined on the purchase portal linked from the product page.
Metered licensing may be used to align costs with document processing volume.
