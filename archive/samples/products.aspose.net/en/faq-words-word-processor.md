## Capabilities and Formats

**Q1. What can I do with the Word Processor plugin?**
The plugin allows you to load, create, edit, and save Word documents programmatically, including operations such as inserting text, tables, images, and sections.
The product page describes these capabilities in the overview content of `content/products.aspose.net/words/en/word-processor/_index.md`.

**Q2. Which document formats are supported?**
According to the product description, supported formats include DOC, DOCX, RTF, DOT, DOTX, DOTM, DOCM, WordML, and several FlatOpc variants.
Additional details are available on the live product page at [products.aspose.net/words/word-processor](https://products.aspose.net/words/word-processor/).

## Integration and Performance

**Q3. How do I integrate the plugin into my .NET project?**
Typical steps include installing the Aspose.Words NuGet package, applying a license, and writing code that works with `Document` and `DocumentBuilder`.
The body section of the product page lists this pattern as a recommended workflow.

**Q4. Is the plugin suitable for server-side processing?**
Yes. The plugin is designed to run in ASP.NET and other server environments without relying on Microsoft Word or other office applications.
This characteristic is emphasized across Aspose marketing and documentation materials.

## Licensing and Samples

**Q5. How is the Word Processor plugin licensed?**
Licensing details are available on the purchase portal linked from the product page, and many deployments use metered licensing for usage-based billing.
Developers can select between traditional and metered options depending on their deployment model.

**Q6. Where can I find sample code?**
The product page and Aspose.Words documentation link to GitHub repositories containing complete examples for common tasks.
These samples demonstrate end-to-end scenarios such as generating reports, converting documents, and applying complex formatting.
