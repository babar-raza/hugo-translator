## General Questions

**Q1. What is the Aspose .NET plugin catalog?**
It is the collection of file format plugins showcased on the products home page, covering documents, spreadsheets, presentations, PDFs, images, barcodes, OCR, and more.
The catalog view described in `content/products.aspose.net/home/en/_index.md` and on [products.aspose.net](https://products.aspose.net/) helps developers choose the right plugin for their workload.

**Q2. Which platforms do these plugins target?**
The catalog focuses on .NET plugins that can be used in ASP.NET, Windows services, desktop applications, and cloud-hosted services.
Plugin descriptions often mention NuGet-based installation and compatibility with modern .NET runtimes.

## Product Families and Scenarios

**Q3. What major plugin families are available?**
Product tiles highlight families such as Aspose.Words, Aspose.Cells, Aspose.PDF, Aspose.Slides, Aspose.Imaging, Aspose.BarCode, and Aspose.OCR.
Each tile links to a dedicated product area under `content/products.aspose.net/<product>/` and the corresponding live product pages.

**Q4. How do I decide which plugin to start with?**
The catalog descriptions and icons indicate target formats and scenarios—for example, document generation vs. spreadsheet automation vs. imaging.
You can follow links to documentation, online demos, and trial downloads from the catalog to validate fit before purchasing.

## Licensing and Pricing

**Q5. Where can I find pricing information?**
Catalog pages include calls to action that link to the Aspose purchase portal, where you can compare license types and costs.
Buttons and links in the products home page (`content/products.aspose.net/home/en/_index.md`) point to these pricing resources.

**Q6. Do all plugins support metered licensing?**
Many .NET plugins support metered licensing, but availability can differ by product.
Licensing details are documented in the product-specific guides and on the purchase portal linked from [products.aspose.net](https://products.aspose.net/).
