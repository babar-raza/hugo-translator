## General Questions

**Q1. What is promoted on the Aspose .NET home pages?**
The home pages promote Aspose .NET plugins and related products for working with file formats such as Word, Excel, PDF, images, and barcodes.
Localized `_index` files describe how these plugins speed up development and automate document tasks.

**Q2. How does the home page relate to the products catalog?**
Home pages highlight key product families and link directly to more detailed pages on products.aspose.net.
This helps visitors move quickly from high-level messaging to specific plugin information.

## Navigation and Localization

**Q3. Are localized versions of the home page available?**
Yes.
The repository includes `_index` files for many languages, such as French, German, and others.
These files adapt the same core messaging for regional audiences.

**Q4. How do I navigate from the home page to documentation and support?**
Navigation elements on the home pages include links to documentation, support forums, and account-related pages.
These links direct visitors to docs.aspose.com, forum.aspose.net, and other key properties.

## Getting Started

**Q5. Where can I view pricing and purchase options?**
Calls to action on the home pages point to the Aspose purchase portal, where you can review pricing tiers and license options.
Buttons like “Get Started” or “Read More” are wired to these purchase or product detail pages.
